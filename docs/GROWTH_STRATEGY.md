# PeeplX Growth Strategy

## Acquiring the First 10,000 Users in Nigeria

---

## Executive Summary

PeeplX targets the **underserved escrow market** in Nigeria's informal digital commerce ecosystem. Our strategy focuses on high-transaction communities that desperately need transaction protection.

**Target:** 10,000 active users within 6 months of launch

### Public Leaderboard

```sql
-- Weekly top traders
SELECT 
  u.username,
  u.avatar_url,
  ts.overall_score,
  ts.total_transactions,
  RANK() OVER (ORDER BY ts.overall_score DESC) as rank
FROM trust_scores ts
JOIN users u ON ts.user_id = u.id
WHERE ts.public_profile_enabled = true
ORDER BY ts.overall_score DESC
LIMIT 100;
```
